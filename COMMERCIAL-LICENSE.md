Geramy Loveless reserves commercial rights in the GLCAD code he owns. The
[source license](LICENSE) permits free noncommercial use. It is a custom
source-available license, not MIT.

A purchase of the official GLCAD App Store application grants the purchaser
permission to use that application, including its later updates, for
commercial CAD work. This permission has no time limit; it ends only if the
purchase is refunded or revoked. The App Store's applicable
installation, account, device, family sharing and payment terms also apply.

Additional features may in future be offered as an optional auto-renewing
subscription. An active paid subscription would grant the entitled subscriber
commercial use of those subscribed features during the paid period, including
an applicable verified billing grace period. It would not withdraw any
permission already granted by a purchase. A separate written agreement with
Geramy Loveless may grant different users, deployments, periods or other
permissions.

This paid permission covers use of the official application to create and
edit drawings, models and deliverables. It does not grant permission to sell
or commercially redistribute GLCAD, commercialize its source or derivatives,
resell purchases or subscriptions, or provide a hosted CAD service. Those
activities need a separate written agreement expressly granting the relevant
rights.

For any subscription, cancelling renewal preserves access through the
remaining valid paid period. When subscribed access ends, commercial creation
and editing with the subscribed features require renewal or another
agreement. Viewing, saving copies of, and exporting existing work remain
available for retrieval. You retain your output, and may continue using
output lawfully created during a valid license period.

The current price, and for a subscription its period and renewal terms, must
appear in the official storefront before purchase. No product identifier or
contact address in a development fixture represents an active commercial
offer. Requests for
other commercial permissions must be directed to Geramy Loveless through the
published support channel when available.

Third-party software keeps its own license terms. Purchasing GLCAD does not
change those terms or grant rights in code that Geramy Loveless does not own.
