2D drafting and 3D modeling in one app

GLCAD is a CAD application for iPad and Mac that puts AutoCAD-style 2D drafting and direct 3D solid modeling on the same document.

  • Sketch and draft with a command line, object snaps, layers, blocks and layouts.
  • Extrude, cut, join and round solids, then dimension them on the same drawing.
  • Open and save DWG and DXF, and export PDF, STL and STEP.
  • Touch to navigate, Apple Pencil to draw, keyboard and mouse when you have them.

Early in development. GLCAD is actively developed and new features are added regularly. See the source repository for what works today and what is coming.
