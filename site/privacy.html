Privacy policy

Effective October 9, 2026

GLCAD is made by Geramy Loveless. This policy covers the GLCAD app for iPad and Mac.

Summary

GLCAD does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it does not send your drawings or usage to the developer. Data leaves your device only when you use a feature that connects to a service you choose.

Your drawings

Drawings, models and settings are stored on your device or in the locations you choose, such as Files or iCloud Drive. The developer has no access to them.

Optional assistant features

The assistant is optional and does nothing until you connect it to an AI server. You choose that server, either by entering its address or by selecting one that GLCAD finds on your local network. When you use the assistant, the requests you make, and the drawing content, images or voice audio needed to answer them, are sent to that server. How that server handles your data is governed by whoever operates it, not by this policy.

If you ask the assistant to search the web or read a page, GLCAD sends the search terms to DuckDuckGo or requests the page from its web site. Those services receive your device's IP address and the request, under their own privacy policies.

Local network

To find AI servers and shared sessions, GLCAD may listen for announcements on your local network. It does not send this information to the developer.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. GLCAD verifies them on your device and does not receive your payment details.

Children

GLCAD is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through support.
